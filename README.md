# 🌾 HexaFarm

### Improving the Farmer Procurement Experience

HexaFarm is a digital platform designed to make the agricultural procurement process **more transparent, accessible, and convenient for farmers**.

The platform focuses on helping farmers find and interact with verified procurement centres, understand procurement requirements, track their procurement status, and reduce unnecessary waiting and uncertainty.

## 🚜 Problem

Farmers often face difficulties during the procurement process, such as:

* Difficulty finding the correct procurement centre
* Lack of clear information about procurement centres
* Long waiting times
* Uncertainty about procurement status
* Lack of transparency in the procurement process
* Difficulty accessing important procurement information
* Confusion regarding required documents and procedures

## 💡 Our Solution

HexaFarm provides a centralized digital platform where farmers can:

* 📍 Find nearby procurement centres
* ✅ View verified procurement-centre information
* 🌾 Check crop/procurement details
* 📋 Understand required documents and procedures
* 🕐 Get information related to waiting/queue status
* 📊 Track procurement progress
* 🔔 Receive important updates and notifications
* 🤖 Get smart assistance for common procurement-related queries

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Module

* Farmer registration/login
* Farmer profile
* Procurement-centre discovery
* Crop and procurement information
* Application/procurement status tracking
* Notifications and updates

### 🏢 Procurement Centre Module

* Centre registration
* Centre verification
* Centre profile
* Procurement capacity/status
* Farmer queue management
* Centre-side updates

### 🛡️ Verification & Transparency

* Verified procurement centres
* Centre information validation
* Transparent status updates
* Reduced chances of fake or misleading centre information

### 📊 Smart Dashboard

The system can provide useful information such as:

* Number of farmers waiting
* Current queue status
* Procurement progress
* Centre availability
* Historical procurement information
